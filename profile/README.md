## 🎮 In Development

### 🏰 Guild & Greed
> **1–10 Player Co-op · Fantasy Dungeon Comedy · Unity 6**

A first-person extraction loop meets a persistent, buildable adventurers' guildhall. Venture into treacherous undercrofts with your party, salvage relics and gold, outsmart hilarious hazards, and extract back to town to upgrade your guild.
- **Format:** 1–10 players, online or LAN multiplayer
- **Current Status:** In active development and internal playtesting
- **Roadmap:** Itch demo release, followed by Steam

### 🚀 Space Game
> **1–4 Player Co-op · Ship Salvage Adventure · Unity 6**

A first-person ship-and-planet salvage adventure for 1–4 friends. Pilot multi-crew vessels, board abandoned derelicts, scavenge valuable components, and survive alien environments together.
- **Format:** 1–4 players, online or LAN multiplayer
- **Playable Prototype:** ▶ **[Play the latest build on itch.io](https://moral-support-studios.itch.io/space-game)**
