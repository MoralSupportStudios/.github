# Code of Conduct

> We've got your back — and we expect you to have everyone else's.

Moral Support Studios is a small studio with an open community. These spaces —
our repos, issues, discussions, and anywhere else we gather — should feel safe
and welcoming for everyone.

## The short version

**Be kind. Be honest. Don't be a jerk.**

## What that means

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree. Critique ideas and
  code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Assume good faith, and give the benefit of the doubt.
- Keep feedback constructive — "this breaks because X, try Y" beats "this is bad."

## Not okay

Harassment, hate speech, slurs, threats, sexual or violent content, doxxing,
sustained disruption, or making people feel unwelcome because of who they are.

## If something goes wrong

Tell us. Email **[Support@MoralSupportStudios.com](mailto:Support@MoralSupportStudios.com)**
or message a maintainer directly. Reports stay confidential. We'll look into it
and do what's needed — up to and including removing people from our spaces.

We'd rather hear about a problem early than find out it festered. We've got
your back.
