# Security Policy

Thanks for helping keep Moral Support Studios and our players safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't** open a public issue for security problems. Instead, email
**[support@moralsupportstudios.com](mailto:support@moralsupportstudios.com)**
with the details and steps to reproduce.

We'll acknowledge your report as soon as we can, keep you posted on the fix, and
credit you once it's resolved if you'd like.

## Scope

This policy covers our published games, our website, and the code in our
repositories. For account or purchase issues on a third-party storefront,
please also contact that platform's support.
