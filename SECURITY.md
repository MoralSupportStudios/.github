# Security Policy

Thanks for helping keep Moral Support Studios and our players safe.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't** open a public issue for security problems. Instead, report it
privately one of these ways:

- Use GitHub's **[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability)**
  on the affected repository (Security → Report a vulnerability), or
- Email **[Support@MoralSupportStudios.com](mailto:Support@MoralSupportStudios.com)**
  with the details and steps to reproduce.

We'll acknowledge your report as soon as we can, keep you posted on the fix, and
credit you once it's resolved if you'd like.

## Scope

This policy covers our published games and the code in our repositories. For
account or purchase issues on third-party storefronts (itch.io and the like),
please also contact that platform's support.
